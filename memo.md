# Executive Recommendation Memo: Guntur Regional Surge

## 1. Title
Operational Escalation & Growth Strategy: Guntur Regional Surge (April–May 2026)

## 2. Context
PharmEast regional operations reviews monthly order data across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Between April 2026 and May 2026, Guntur demonstrated an exceptional order value and sales increase, surging by +122.19% from INR 105,420.00 to INR 234,180.00.

## 3. Key Insight
Guntur's sales surge was propelled predominantly by an increase in Prescription Medicines and Wellness & Nutrition orders, defying the broader regional flat trend and bucking Visakhapatnam's seasonal dip [HIGH].

## 4. Evidence
- **April 2026 Sales**: INR 105,420.00 across 63 orders [HIGH].
- **May 2026 Sales**: INR 234,180.00 across 141 orders [HIGH].
- **MoM Growth Rate**: +122.19%, the largest absolute and percentage magnitude movement across all 9 active regions [HIGH].

## 5. Recommendation
Deploy immediate fulfillment and inventory buffering support to Guntur hub to maintain delivery SLAs and prevent stockouts on high-velocity pharmaceutical SKUs [MEDIUM].

## 6. Next Check
Re-evaluate Guntur performance metrics in the June 2026 closing report to verify whether this growth plateaus or stabilizes at the higher baseline [LOW].

## 7. Assumptions
It is hypothesized that local promotional campaigns or sudden epidemiological shifts drove increased healthcare demand in Guntur during May 2026, though specific external marketing attribution data is outside the scope of current order logs [MEDIUM].
